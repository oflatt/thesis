\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

% Sources: report thesis and project sections; new concluding synthesis.
\sourcenote{New synthesis of the report's semantic and algorithmic contributions.}
This thesis develops e-graph extensions for reasoning about and transforming programs:
  proof production, effect-safe extraction, and composable support for analyses and binding.
Proofs enable checking, explanation, and debugging;
  effect-safe extraction makes the selected program executable with a valid effect ordering.
The proposed verified compiler brings these capabilities together
  and will test their combined benefit for compilation.

% Sources: Small Proofs, Sections 4--5; eggcc, Sections 1 and 3.
\sourcenote{New synthesis of the parameterized-algorithm contribution.}
Parameterized algorithms provide a recurring method for making these extensions efficient.
For proof minimization, both \textsc{TreeOpt} and greedy proof search are parameterized
  by the number of congruence edges.
Bounding this count and the number of recursive searches makes the greedy algorithm practical.
For effect-safe extraction, statewalk width captures the interactions
  that the algorithm must handle and remains small on the evaluated workloads.
Identifying these structural parameters connects complexity analysis to algorithm design
  and gives the proposed compiler evaluation concrete explanations to test.
