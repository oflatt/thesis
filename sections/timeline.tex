\section{Dissertation Plan and Timeline}
\label{sec:timeline}

% Source: Oliver's schedule, November 20, 2026: cleanup and the egglog journal
% paper until March; begin remaining work in March; graduate around May 2028.
% Chapter organization follows the report's completed and proposed projects.
\sourcenote{New: dates from Oliver's plan; proposed chapter organization.}
The dissertation will develop the work in Sections~\ref{sec:proofs}--\ref{sec:extensions}
  into chapters on proof minimization, effect-safe extraction, and composable extensions.
A fourth research chapter will present the verified-compilation library and its case study.
Work on that project begins in March 2027, following cleanup of existing work
  and work on the \texttt{egglog} journal paper.
The target for dissertation completion and graduation is May 2028.
